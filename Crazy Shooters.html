Crazy Shooters

By OnlineGames.io

About Crazy Shooters

Crazy Shooters is a free online multiplayer first-person shooter where you build your own match from scratch — name a room, set the player limit, and choose Free-For-All or Team mode before dropping onto one of four combat maps. Pick from nine distinct weapons, adapt fast to whatever range the fight puts you at, and prove you can out-shoot and out-move everyone else in the room.

Crazy Shooters runs entirely in-browser with no download required, and its room-based system means you can just as easily host your own private match with friends as jump into someone else's public game. A full in-match statistics screen tracks your games played, wins, kills, and score, so your progress carries meaning across every session.

Maps and Weapons

Crazy Shooters includes four maps — Factory, Arena, Towers, and GasPlant — each offering different sightlines, cover, and pacing, from tight indoor corridors to wide-open arenas. Your arsenal spans nine weapons, including the BallisticKnife, Deagle, MP5KA4, STW-25, M87T, Blaser R93, RPG, M79, and M67, giving you room to specialize in close-range knife rushes, mid-range assault rifle fights, or long-range precision kills.

Rooms, Modes, and Stats

Every match starts with a room: name it, cap the player count, and choose Free-For-All for a chaotic every-soldier-for-themselves fight or Team mode to coordinate with allies. The HUD keeps you informed mid-match with a kill feed, current weapon display, health and ammo readout, and a running match timer, while a dedicated statistics screen tallies your total games played, wins, kills, and overall score after every session.

Tips for Winning at Crazy Shooters

Learn each map's chokepoints before committing to a weapon loadout — a sniper-friendly weapon is far less useful on the tight corridors of Factory than in the open sightlines of Arena. Keep moving between engagements so you're not an easy target for a second enemy rotating in, and always top off your ammo with R during quiet moments rather than mid-firefight.

How to Play Crazy Shooters

  1. Create or join a room — Name your own room and set a player limit, or join an existing one.
  2. Pick your mode — Choose Free-For-All to fight everyone, or Team mode to fight alongside allies.
  3. Choose a map — Drop into Factory, Arena, Towers, or GasPlant.
  4. Select your weapon — Pick from nine weapons ranging from knives to rocket launchers.
  5. Engage the enemy — Move fast, use cover, and land your shots before you're spotted.
  6. Track your progress — Check the statistics screen after each match to see your kills, wins, and score.
